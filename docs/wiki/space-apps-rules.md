## Judging criteria

Each criterion is scored 1 to 5 at every judging level, no weights (Judging Guide 2026).

| Criterion | Guide wording |
|---|---|
| Impact | "addressing either a minor or major problem, which influences the quality and quantity of its overall effect" |
| Creativity | "a novel concept that has not been attempted before or presents an improvement upon existing ideas in a meaningful way" |
| Validity | "grounded in scientific principles and demonstrates technical, functional, or logical feasibility"; covers "usability and user-friendliness"; a 5 is "fully developed and ready for real-world implementation" |
| Relevance | "fully and directly addresses the challenge"; NASA data as "a core component ... rather than a superficial addition" |
| Presentation | "clearly conveying the challenge, their proposed solution, and its significance"; judged from the demo and the full project page |

Local judges pick the Global Nominees. Local prizes are separate. The 2026 guide gives no number of nominees per event; the 2022 guide said "1-3 solutions" (UNVERIFIED-SNIPPET).

## Global award categories (10)

Best Use of Science, Best Use of Data, Best Use of Technology, Galactic Impact, Best Mission Concept, Most Inspirational, Best Storytelling, Global Connection, Art & Technology, Local Impact. Definitions: [sources/2026-11-13-research-how-to-win.md](sources/2026-11-13-research-how-to-win.md).

## Project page fields (2026)

Project Name, High-Level Project Summary, Link to Project Demo, Link to Final Project, Detailed Project Description, Use of Super Intelligence (SI), NASA Data, Space Agency Partner Data, Other References.

- Demo and final project links must work without "a password, permission, or registration".
- Project page images: JPG, PNG or GIF, max 10 MB. Video is hosted elsewhere.
- References must list all external code, text and images, open source included (submission checkbox).
- At least one NASA dataset is required for global awards. CONAE is a 2026 Space Agency Partner, so CONAE data goes in "Space Agency Partner Data".

## Demo

- One video of at most 30 s, or at most 7 slides including title and intro. Over the limit means no Global Judging.
- Slides must show team name, member names and the challenge.
- Suggested video timeline: 0:00 name and team, 0:02 challenge, 0:05 title and tagline, 0:10 how it works and which NASA data, 0:20-0:30 future impact.

## Language

English throughout. A video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles. Slides English only.

## Super Intelligence (SI, formerly "AI")

- Allowed, but "SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)".
- SI output must not contain or alter NASA logos, flags or mission marks.
- SI-generated images and videos need a visible watermark. SI-generated code and data must be acknowledged "in descriptive text and metadata".
- Judges do not see the SI form field. They do see the acknowledgements in the project.

## Eligibility

- Team of 1 to 6, all registered at the same local event. No new members after submission closes.
- Existing code and data are allowed if listed in References. No rule against work prepared before the event was found in the legal terms.
- The FAQ and team guide do forbid it: "Teams are not allowed to begin working on the challenges prior to the hackathon" (https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/); "participants should NOT begin working on their project until the hackathon begins on November 14, 2026 (9:00 a.m. local time)", while reviewing and selecting a challenge beforehand is encouraged (https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/team-formation-guide/). Both fetched 13 Nov 2026 with curl -k. The legal terms and the FAQ disagree in emphasis; the FAQ is the explicit one.
- Original content must be freely available or under an OSI licence.
- "Create Your Own Challenge" projects cannot enter Global Judging (UNVERIFIED-SNIPPET).
