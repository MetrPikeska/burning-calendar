# Storylines (30 s global judging video)

Picked by Petr on 14 Nov 2026: **A**.

## A. Fake jump (picked)

Device: a number lands. Form: one frame that rebuilds (the chart) then a real demo.
The viewer sees Greece's yearly fire detections jump in 2012, learns the jump is a sensor change, watches the harmonized line close it, then sees the app answer one real question (Entre Ríos, Aug 2020, 26x the median).
First 2 s: the chart already on screen, the MODIS line drawing towards 2012.
Beats: jump -> question -> "the satellite changed" -> harmonized line closes the gap -> app demo -> two validation numbers -> end card.
Look: the app's own warm paper ground, IBM Plex Sans, inferno ramp. Sound: none in the rough cut.

## B. Analyst's question

Device: a moment in someone's day. A fire analyst asks "Is this October normal for Kentriki Makedonia?"; the app answers with the calendar and the median (Oct 2026 data), then an AI agent answers the same question over MCP.
First 2 s: the question typed on a phone-sized card over the map.
Beats: question -> app answer -> agent answer -> numbers -> end card.

## C. Orbit to calendar

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: an endless zoom.
A Higgsfield-generated satellite over Earth (watermarked) dives into the Greece map, the calendar heatmap unfolds year by year and the 2012 seam closes.
First 2 s: generated orbit shot. Most cinematic, least concrete, costs credits.
